    How to play

    How to play

    Guess today's hidden One Piece character in 8 tries. Characters go up to the Elbaf arc.

    • Pick a character. Start typing a name and choose from the list.
    • Read the tiles. Each guess shows seven clues comparing your character with the hidden one.
    • Narrow it down and solve it in 8 guesses to keep your streak.
    • Share your grid in the group chat. It shows how you did without giving the answer away.

    Colours

    • Green: exact match.
    • Yellow: close.
    • Red: no match.
    • ↑ means the hidden character is taller, has a higher bounty or first appeared later. ↓ means the opposite.

    The seven clues

    • Gender: green if the same.
    • Height: green if exact, yellow if within 15 cm, with arrows.
    • Haki: O = Observation, A = Armament, C = Conqueror's. Green if exactly the same, yellow if at least one in common.
    • Fruit: Paramecia, Zoan, Logia or None. Green if the same, yellow if both have a Devil Fruit of different types. Blackbeard counts as both Logia and Paramecia.
    • Bounty: green if exact, red otherwise, with arrows. "Unknown" counts as 0, so Unknown against None shows yellow.
    • Affiliation: Pirate, Gov't, Both or Other. Yellow if one is "Both" and the other is Pirate or Gov't.
    • First arc: green if the same arc, yellow if a different arc in the same saga, with arrows.

    One puzzle a day, the same for everyone, resetting at midnight on your device.

    About the facts. Some details, such as certain heights, bounties and Haki, are estimates based on story information because they haven't been officially confirmed. Marine bounties are fan estimates based on rank and fame. If you spot something wrong, say so and it'll be fixed.
